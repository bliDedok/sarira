# Expert Validation Required

Sebelum production diperlukan review nutrition/product/content untuk:

- Food dataset dan serving conversions.
- Recipe composition, steps, food safety wording, allergens, dietary/halal verification.
- Target policies Phase 5 dan critical nutrients.
- Candidate weights, thresholds, fit labels, snack policy, replacement equivalence.
- Suggestion priority, protein/fiber candidates, sodium/sugar/saturated-fat wording.
- Teen, healthy-aging, yellow/red safety behavior.
- Estimated cost, time, equipment metadata, accessibility copy, dan consent policy.

Status development tidak boleh dipromosikan sebagai clinical optimization, validated recipe, guaranteed outcome, atau medical prescription.
